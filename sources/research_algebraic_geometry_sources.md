# Research notes: algebraic geometry essay

Primary references consulted on 2026-08-12:

- The Stacks Project, “The spectrum of a ring”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DY
  - Definition of `Spec(A)`, Zariski closed sets `V(I)`, standard opens `D(f)`, and dependence of `V(I)` only on the radical.
- The Stacks Project, “Affine schemes”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01HU
  - Definition of the structure sheaf and affine scheme as a locally ringed space.
- The Stacks Project, “The category of affine schemes”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01HX
  - Equivalence between affine schemes and the opposite of rings; affine fiber products as spectra of tensor products.
- The Stacks Project, “Points of schemes”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01J5
  - Functor of points and the description of maps from spectra of local rings.
- The Stacks Project, “Tangent spaces”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B28
  - Tangent vectors via points valued in dual numbers.
- The Stacks Project, “Computing intersection multiplicities”: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B01
  - Under suitable Cohen–Macaulay hypotheses, local intersection multiplicity equals the length of the local ring of the scheme-theoretic intersection.
- Ravi Vakil, *The Rising Sea: Foundations of Algebraic Geometry*: https://math.stanford.edu/~vakil/216blog/FOAGaug2922publici.pdf
  - Nilpotents do not alter the underlying topological space but remain in the structure sheaf; dual numbers encode differential information.

The article uses the line–parabola tangency as its original expository spine. Definitions and categorical formulas were checked against the sources above.
